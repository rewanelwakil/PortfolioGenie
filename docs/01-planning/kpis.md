# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| # | KPI | Description | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Portfolio generation time | Time from submitting the form to seeing the generated portfolio | Under 30 seconds |
| 2 | Page load time | Time for the builder and preview pages to load | Under 3 seconds |
| 3 | Form completion rate | Percentage of users who finish the multi-step form | At least 70% |
| 4 | AI suggestion acceptance rate | Percentage of AI-generated descriptions accepted by users | At least 60% |
| 5 | System uptime | Percentage of time the application is available | At least 99% |
